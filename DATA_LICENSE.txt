The corpus is derived from real-human LibriVox audiobook recordings and
Project Gutenberg texts. Recording paths retained in each manifest provide
source-book and speaker provenance. Only the clean subset is selected.

Modifications in this project: selection of speakers 92 and 6097, approximately
15 training hours per speaker; filtering by duration and signal checks;
resampling from 44,100 Hz to 24,000 Hz with soxr VHQ; attenuation of resampling
peaks above 0.99 to prevent PCM clipping; PCM encoding; acoustic
feature extraction; phoneme forced alignment and alignment-quality filtering.
The original development and test splits are retained, with duplicate texts
removed across splits. The TTS model is initialized from scratch.

The pretrained wav2vec2 alignment model is used for offline annotation only;
it does not generate training audio or initialize the TTS model.
The dizione phonemizer uses CMUdict-based lexical pronunciation tables, not
synthetic speech. Their original license notices are retained in phonemizer/.
